## Goal

One or two sentences: what the child must produce.

## Why

The reason for the work, so the child can make sensible choices the brief does not cover.

## Scope

In scope:

- What the child changes or investigates.

Out of scope:

- What the child must leave alone.

## References

Links, tickets, PRs and absolute file paths the child needs.

## Done when

The checks that prove the work is finished, each one something the child can verify.
